# Enthalpy is a State Function

Our expression for internal energy at constant pressure:

$$ \Delta U=q_P+w=q_P-P\Delta V $$

Rearrange:

$$ q_P=\Delta U+P\Delta V=U_2-U_1+P(V_2-V_1) $$

$$ q_P=\left(U_2+PV_2\right)-\left(U_1+PV_1\right) $$

We can define this term as enthalpy:

$$ H\equiv U+PV $$

This is a new state function.
