Pressure and the enthalpy

How does pressure affect enthalpy $\kHenth$? As we showed above we have the following relations of first and second order for $\kG$

$$ \left( \dfrac{\partial \kG}{\partial \kT} \right)_{\kP} = -\kSent $$ $$ \left( \dfrac{\partial \kG}{\partial \kP} \right)_{\kT} = -\kV $$ $$ -\left (\dfrac{\partial \kSent}{\partial \kP }\right)_{\kT} = \left (\dfrac{\partial \kV}{\partial \kT} \right)_{\kP} $$

As we can see for an ideal gas, there is no dependence of $\kHenth$ on $\kP$.
